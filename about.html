Who I Am

Hi, I’m Edén.

I’ve always been drawn to music, poetry, philosophy, and long conversations about how we understand the world and our place in it. Curiosity has probably been the most consistent thread throughout my life — sometimes expressed through art, sometimes through science, and often through simply asking too many questions.

Music has been a constant companion. Playing the guitar is one of my favorite ways to disconnect, reflect, and sometimes make sense of things that are difficult to put into words. I’ve always felt that music and poetry have a unique way of capturing emotions and ideas that logic alone can’t fully explain.

I also volunteer as a firefighter in Villars-sur-Glâne, in the canton of Fribourg. It’s an experience that keeps me grounded and reminds me how important teamwork, responsibility, and calm decision-making can be. It’s also a meaningful way for me to give back to the community that has become my home.

I grew up in the Valencian Community in Spain, where my curiosity about how things work first started to take shape. That curiosity eventually led me toward science and technology, and later brought me to Switzerland — a place that has deeply influenced both my personal life and professional path.

The Swiss mountains quickly became one of my favorite places to be. Whether skiing in Schwarzsee, hiking near Jungfraujoch, or enjoying the light and landscapes around Anzère, nature is where I recharge and reconnect. Photography started as a way to capture these moments, but it soon turned into a passion on its own. I enjoy searching for details, light, and perspectives that tell small stories within larger landscapes.

Reading is another important part of my daily life, especially books related to philosophy, history, and society. I’m fascinated by how technology shapes human behavior and how data and innovation can be used to improve people’s lives, particularly in healthcare.

Professionally, I work in data engineering, building systems that transform complex data into meaningful information. But this page is not really about that. It’s about the other parts of my life — the interests, experiences, and passions that shape how I think, learn, and interact with the world.

My Passions

Alpine Skiing

The Swiss Alps offer some of the world's best skiing resorts, and I try to hit the slopes whenever I can. There's nothing quite like the feeling of carving fresh tracks in powder snow, with mountain peaks stretching endlessly around you. Schwarzsee, Les Diablerets, or Porte du Soleil are some of my favorite resort.

Photography

I love capturing the world through my lens—from sweeping mountain landscapes to intimate street scenes. Photography teaches you to see the world differently, to notice the light, the composition, the story in every frame. It's meditation and adventure rolled into one.

Poetry & Literature

I find solace and inspiration in the written word, particularly poetry. Khalil Gibran's work resonates deeply with me—his ability to capture profound truths in simple, beautiful language speaks to both the analytical and contemplative sides of my nature. Reading is my way of exploring the human experience beyond data and algorithms.
