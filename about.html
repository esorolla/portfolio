Beyond the Code

Adventures, passions, and the moments in between

Who I Am

When I'm not architecting data pipelines or diving into complex datasets, you'll likely find me exploring the mountains of Switzerland, capturing moments through my camera lens, or reading and writing. Life, for me, is about balance—the precision of engineering and the spontaneity of adventure.

Growing up in Valencian Community, Spain, I developed an early fascination with understanding how things work. That curiosity eventually brought me to Switzerland, where I've not only built a career in technology but also discovered a deep love for the Alpine landscape. Whether it's skiing down pristine slopes in Schwarzsee, hiking through Jungfraujoch, or capturing the golden hour light in Anzère, the mountains have become my second home.

Photography started as a way to document these adventures, but it quickly became a passion of its own. There's something about freezing a moment in time—the play of light on snow, the vastness of a mountain vista, the quiet beauty of everyday scenes—that speaks to the same part of me that loves solving complex problems in Data Engineering. Both require patience, attention to detail, and the ability to see patterns others might miss.

Beyond skiing and photography, I'm an avid reader, a guitar player amateur, and someone who believes that the best conversations happen over a good meal with friends. I'm fascinated by the intersection of technology and society, and I enjoy exploring how data can be used not just to optimize systems, but to genuinely improve people's lives—especially in healthcare.

This page is a glimpse into the parts of my life that don't fit neatly into a CV. It's the adventures, the hobbies, the moments that remind me why I do what I do. Because at the end of the day, we're not just building systems and pipelines—we're living full, interesting lives.

My Passions

Alpine Skiing

The Swiss Alps offer some of the world's best skiing resorts, and I try to hit the slopes whenever I can. There's nothing quite like the feeling of carving fresh tracks in powder snow, with mountain peaks stretching endlessly around you. Schwarzsee, Les Diablerets, or Porte du Soleil are some of my favorite resort.

Photography

I love capturing the world through my lens—from sweeping mountain landscapes to intimate street scenes. Photography teaches you to see the world differently, to notice the light, the composition, the story in every frame. It's meditation and adventure rolled into one.

Poetry & Literature

I find solace and inspiration in the written word, particularly poetry. Khalil Gibran's work resonates deeply with me—his ability to capture profound truths in simple, beautiful language speaks to both the analytical and contemplative sides of my nature. Reading is my way of exploring the human experience beyond data and algorithms.
